November 11, 2026

You’re one of Vault’s first 50

The first Vault newsletter went to the first 50 people who signed up for the Vault MCP. Lee recorded a 1:44 video hello on why Vault exists: free bank and credit union data, from public filings, shaped by the people who use it.

What was new:

  • Branch-level activity. Every branch and its deposits, with county market share.
  • History that goes back decades, not two years. Call-report financials to 1992 and branch history to 1994.
  • Credit unions in the MCP. Search now covers banks and credit unions, with a new credit union profile tool. 18 tools in total.

The ask was simple: reply with the one thing you wish Vault did. Those replies are shaping what ships next, including a Microsoft Copilot listing that’s in progress.
